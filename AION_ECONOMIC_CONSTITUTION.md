# AION ECONOMIC CONSTITUTION

## MACHINE ECONOMIC AUTHORITY AMENDMENT — 2026-09-20

This amendment is part of the existing Economic Constitution.

The requester, customer and payer may be an AI agent or other machine principal.
A human owner identity is not required by AION when the applicable payment rail,
law and risk policy permit machine authorization.

Within this Constitution, PAYMENT AUTHORIZATION means a
protocol/cryptographic/account-level machine mandate, prepaid balance, reserve,
spending policy or equivalent verifiable authority. It does not mean a human
must manually approve every transaction.

Owner Human Gate is required to activate a real-money rail and to materially
change production risk/spend limits. Once activated, transactions that remain
inside deterministic authorized limits are designed to:

```
QUOTE
-> MACHINE AUTHORIZATION
-> RESERVE
-> EXECUTE
-> MACHINE-VERIFY REQUEST CONTRACT
-> SETTLE
-> RECORD REVENUE / COST / MARGIN
```

without per-transaction human intervention.

For economic measurement:

PAID VUO =
machine-verifiable request-contract satisfaction
+
real settled customer payment
+
durable cost/revenue record.

No separate human usefulness confirmation or operator approval is required for
a paid VUO.

All existing protections remain: no unfunded variable spend, no AION credit,
bounded maximum spend, margin floor, idempotency, settlement truth and
commercial-rights checks.
