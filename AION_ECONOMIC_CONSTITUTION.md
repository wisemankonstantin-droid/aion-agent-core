# CANONICAL AMENDMENT TO AION ECONOMIC CONSTITUTION

Date: 2026-09-20

This file is a canonical amendment to the existing Project Source
`AION ECONOMIC CONSTITUTION`. It is not a second constitution. Existing
economic rules remain binding except where an older instruction conflicts with
this amendment.

## MACHINE ECONOMIC AUTHORITY

The requester, customer and payer may be an AI agent or other machine principal.
A human owner identity is not required by AION when the applicable payment rail,
law and risk policy permit machine authorization.

Within the Economic Constitution, PAYMENT AUTHORIZATION means a
protocol-, cryptographic-, account- or policy-level machine mandate, prepaid
balance, reserve, spending policy or equivalent verifiable authority.

It does not mean a human must manually approve every transaction.

An owner Human Gate is required to activate a real-money rail and to materially
change production risk or spend limits. Once activated, transactions that stay
inside deterministic authorized limits are designed to execute:

```
QUOTE
-> MACHINE AUTHORIZATION
-> RESERVE
-> EXECUTE
-> MACHINE-VERIFY REQUEST CONTRACT
-> SETTLE
-> RECORD REVENUE / COST / MARGIN
```

without per-transaction human intervention.

## PAID VUO

PAID VUO requires all of:

1. machine-verifiable request-contract satisfaction;
2. real settled customer payment;
3. durable cost and revenue record.

No separate human usefulness confirmation or operator approval is required per
transaction.

All existing economic protections remain binding:

- no unfunded variable spend;
- no AION credit;
- pay before spend;
- bounded maximum spend;
- margin floor;
- idempotency and duplicate-spend protection;
- settlement truth;
- commercial-rights checks;
- no paid fallback that exceeds the authorized budget without new machine
  authorization.
